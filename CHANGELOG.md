# Changelog

## 0.7.0 - 2026-10-27

A redesigned window, profiles, and new patches.

- Window: a new layout with patches grouped into categories.
- Profiles: Clarity, Balanced, and Performance replace the old presets. Picking one replaces your selection.
- Atmosphere: Fog, Rain, Clouds, and Env particles are now one patch. Saved selections move over on their own.
- Screen filters: a new patch that turns off vignette, depth of field, and the desaturation filter for chill and low life.
- Compact mod lines: a new patch that shortens the affix and tier lines on Alt tooltips. English client only.
- Minimap: adjustable reveal strength, and floor and wall colors.
- MTX effects: town portals stay visible by default. Turn on "Include town portals" to strip them too.
- Color mods: waystone mods with hidden item rarity are now green, and waystone mods that had no color now have one. Belts are no longer colored by mistake. Click "Reset to defaults" in the color editor to get the new colors. Mods that weren't in your list are added on their own.
- The footer now shows errors from Apply and Restore.
- A new app icon. The Windows `.exe` now shows it in Explorer and on shortcuts too.
- Build: Rust 1.99.1 and edition 2024.
